Matte Painter in Toronto — Pat Lau

Digital Environments for Film & Television

Pat Lau is a senior digital matte painter based in Toronto, Ontario. He paints digital environments for feature films, television series, commercials and game cinematics, and works with productions in Toronto, Montreal and Vancouver.

Feature film and television

His most recent matte painting on this site is the New Asia City night environment for Gareth Edwards’ The Creator, painted at MARZ VFX, along with a matte painting for The Walking Dead: Dead City, also at MARZ VFX. Earlier feature work includes Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon: Sword of Destiny (dir. Yuen Woo-ping) at Pixomondo, Garm Wars: The Last Druid (dir. Mamoru Oshii) at Intelligent Creatures, and Resident Evil: Retribution 3D (dir. Paul W. S. Anderson) at Mr. X. For television he painted environments for Orphan Black at Intelligent Creatures and an abandoned shopping-mall interior for a TV show at Inversion Studio.

His résumé also lists studio credits at WeFX (John Wick: Chapter 4, The Fall of the House of Usher, Reacher S2), MARZ VFX (Wednesday, Moon Knight, WandaVision, Umbrella Academy 3) and Pixomondo Toronto (Star Trek: Discovery, Wonder Woman, The Fate of the Furious).

Short films, commercials and games

Short-film matte paintings include Azul (dir. David Karlak), Law Zero (dir. Macgregor) and Nuit Blanche (dir. Arev Manoukian, Stellar Scene), where he was senior digital matte painter. Commercial work includes Hyundai Genesis (Dashing), Nissan Versa Note (The Vanity) and Maple Leaf Canadian Craft (POPBOX VFX). For games he painted environments for Star Wars: Uprising and Star Wars: Commander at Goldtooth Creative.

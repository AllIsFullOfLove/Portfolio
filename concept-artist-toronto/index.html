Concept Artist in Toronto — Pat Lau

Environments · Characters · Props for Film, TV & Games

Pat Lau is a concept artist based in Toronto, Ontario. He designs environments, characters, armour and props for feature films, television, short films and games, and works with productions in Toronto, Montreal and Vancouver.

Feature films

Feature-film concept work includes Colossal (2016, dir. Nacho Vigalondo), the Æon Flux city design (internal concept design at MARZ), Downsizing (2017, Alexander Payne, Paramount) and Garm Wars: The Last Druid (dir. Mamoru Oshii, Intelligent Creatures). For Tsui Hark he designed for The Monkey King, and his armour and weapon designs for Seven Swords were shown at the Film Workshop 25th Anniversary Exhibition at agnès b. in Hong Kong.

Television and short films

For television he has designed for DC’s Titans (Starfire, DC Entertainment), Heroes Reborn, In Contempt and Brain City. Short-film work includes the cyberpunk apartment interior for Shut In (dir. Joseph Procopio, Diamond Box Entertainment), concept design for Concrete City (dir. Arev Manoukian) and character look development for an untitled short film at Intelligent Creatures.

Games and interactive

Game and interactive work includes Project Scorpion for Xbox, the “Wraith Vision” design of the Nine Nazgûl on fell beasts for Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor at Goldtooth Creative, and the Horror Cam concept for Google’s Project Tango with Jam3.

What the work covers

The concept section of the portfolio spans city and environment design (Æon Flux, Colossal, Brain City), interior design (Shut In), character design and character look development, armour and weapon design (Seven Swords), and the Nazgûl “Wraith Vision” design for the game Shadow of Mordor. Most projects show several views or variations, and every image opens full size.

Concept art that goes all the way to the shot

Pat is also a matte painter, compositor and VFX supervisor, so the same artist can take an idea from concept painting through to a finished environment. See his matte painting and visual effects pages.

Storyboard Artist in Toronto — Pat Lau

Commercials · Music Videos · Film

Pat Lau is a storyboard artist based in Toronto, Ontario, who draws boards for TV commercials, music videos, feature films and branded projects. He works with productions in Toronto, Montreal and Vancouver.

Commercials and branded work

Storyboards on this site include the Sony 3D — Two Worlds TV commercial (dir. Arev Manoukian), the Nokia commercial The Passenger, and storyboards for TextEd.ca and for Msheireb Properties’ Downtown Doha.

Music videos and film

Music-video boards include “500”, and on the Skrillex ft. Wolfgang Gartner video The Devil’s Den at Spy Films he worked as storyboard artist and digital illustrator. Film boards include the feature Twins Effect 2 and rough internal boards for an untitled action short.

From rough boards to client presentations

The storyboards range from quick, rough internal boards used to plan an action sequence to finished frames for commercial clients. The Sony 3D — Two Worlds section of the portfolio shows the boards next to the finished commercial, so you can compare the drawings with the final film.

Browse the boards

Every storyboard on this site opens full size — click a frame below, or browse the storyboard section of the portfolio. Projects shown there: Sony 3D — Two Worlds, Nokia — The Passenger, “500”, Twins Effect 2, an untitled action short, TextEd.ca and Msheireb Properties — Downtown Doha.

Boards from someone who also does the VFX

Storyboarding has been part of Pat’s work throughout his career: his résumé lists storyboard artist alongside VFX supervisor and senior VFX compositor at Parktown Productions and at Fuel Industries / Fuel Films / Karbon Arc. He is also a VFX supervisor, matte painter and concept artist, so his boards are drawn by someone who knows how the shots will be built.

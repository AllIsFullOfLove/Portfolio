VFX Supervisor in Toronto — Pat Lau

Visual Effects · Compositing · Supervision

Pat Lau is a visual effects supervisor, compositor and matte painter based in Toronto, Ontario. His résumé lists him as VFX supervisor, senior VFX compositor and storyboard artist at Parktown Productions, and as VFX supervisor, senior VFX compositor, storyboard artist and senior matte painter at Fuel Industries / Fuel Films / Karbon Arc.

Visual effects on this site

The visual effects section of the portfolio includes the Meteor Project VFX breakdown; the Skrillex ft. Wolfgang Gartner music video The Devil’s Den (dir. Arev Manoukian, Spy Films), where he was storyboard artist and digital illustrator; and the music video ERUPT — The Art of Beat Making (dir. Will Cyr, Secret Level Films). The Devil’s Den page lets you switch each still between the final frame and its “before” version.

From storyboard to final composite

Because Pat also works as a storyboard artist, concept artist and matte painter, he can plan a sequence on paper, design and paint its environments, and supervise and composite the shots.
